# Citation and attribution

If JoJo contributes to your research, cite the Green-theorem derivation described in [Liu et al. (2024)](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.3847/1538-3881/ad9b8c). For the retained spherical occultation implementation, see [Eastman, Gaudi & Agol (2013)](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2013PASP..125...83E/abstract).

JoJo's source, license, and issue tracker are available in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Flippedx/JoJo).
